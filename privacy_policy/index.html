Privacy Policy

App: Screen Rotation (com.screenrotator3.app)
Developer: MintLeaf Studio
Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Screen Rotation ("the App") handles information when you use it. We designed the App to work entirely on your device and to collect as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or personally identifiable information. It does not require an account, login, or registration.

The App only uses the device's orientation and display settings that are necessary to provide its core function. These settings are processed locally on your device and are not sent to us or to any third party.

2. Permissions

The App may request the "WRITE_SETTINGS" or related system settings permission solely to change the screen orientation. This permission is used only for that purpose, and no device state is uploaded or shared.

3. Ads and Analytics

The App contains no advertisements and no third-party advertising SDKs. We do not use analytics services that identify you or track your activity across apps or websites.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose any user data to third parties, because the App does not collect any user data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

6. Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted by the App, there is no user data stored on our servers that could be compromised.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
